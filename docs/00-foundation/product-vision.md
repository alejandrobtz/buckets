# Product Vision

> **Status:** Draft · **Last updated:** 2026-11-02

## Vision

Buckets is a financial operating system for people and households. It helps them move from *recording transactions* to *understanding, planning and deciding*.

## Guiding questions

| Question | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Know** | What do I have, earn, spend and owe — and how much can I trust that data? |
| **Plan** | What do I want money to do over upcoming periods? |
| **Decide** | Given my situation, what are my options and their trade-offs? |
| **Review & learn** | What happened versus what I expected, and what should change? |

## Who it serves

- People who only want to log income and expenses and see balances. **Tracker-only is a valid permanent mode**, not a stepping stone.
- People who additionally want purposeful allocation (Buckets), goals, strategies and commitments, adopted gradually and optionally.
- Households sharing finances.

## Non-goals (for now)

- Moving money or acting as a bank.
- Replacing a bank's records: Buckets is a decision-support layer.
- Autonomous AI financial decisions.

How these ideas are applied is defined in [product-principles.md](product-principles.md).
